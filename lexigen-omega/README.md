# LEXIGEN Ω — Open-Ended Semantic Discovery

Status: **development track started; no AGI or breakthrough claim**.

LEXIGEN Ω is the algorithm/scientific-discovery engine intended to support a broader general-intelligence architecture. It is not another hand-authored Lexigen V8/V9 rescue. The track is isolated from the frozen V7 GSO campaign and older Language Genesis branches.

## Core hypothesis

A discovery system can generalize more broadly if it can:

1. express candidate solutions in a small task-agnostic executable substrate;
2. invent reusable executable semantic primitives from lower-level programs;
3. evolve programs and search policies under hard evaluators;
4. retain a learned primitive only after prospective causal-transfer evidence;
5. attack retained mechanisms with counterexamples and ablations;
6. recursively build higher-order mechanisms from mechanisms that survived those tests.

The decisive distinction from prior Lexigen lines is that language growth is part of the algorithm. Human-authored finished-task operators are not allowed into the universal substrate after blind evaluation begins.

## Non-negotiable scientific rules

- Frozen V7 and historical negative evidence is preserved, not rewritten.
- Development tasks are explicitly contaminated and may be used aggressively before the final freeze.
- Holdout identities are selected only after the Ω engine, semantic memory, adapters, budgets, selectors, seeds, and gates are frozen.
- No task replacement after task/spec access.
- No post-result threshold changes.
- No hidden expert patch/hint leakage before final candidate freeze.
- Every claimed causal-transfer win requires full vs no-memory vs equal-budget random-memory controls plus exact semantic-gene removal/ablation.
- A stored semantic gene is not evidence of generality by itself.
- A global breakthrough claim requires a larger heterogeneous denominator, independent reproduction, and at least one genuinely new external-frontier result.

## Architecture

Ω is being built as ten interacting layers:

1. **Universal Substrate** — typed values, collections, graphs/tensors, state, control flow, functions, constraints and evaluators.
2. **Semantic Genesis** — derives executable semantic genes from lower-level programs.
3. **Program Evolution** — population search over executable candidates.
4. **Language Evolution** — changes representation/abstraction while preserving executable semantics.
5. **Searcher Evolution** — improves decomposition, proposal, mutation and scheduling policies on meta-holdouts.
6. **Causal Memory** — admits only mechanisms with prospective causal evidence.
7. **Counterexample Engine** — generates adversarial cases and falsifies over-general abstractions.
8. **Verifier Co-Synthesis** — learns screening contracts while retaining independent soundness anchors.
9. **Open-Ended Archive** — preserves diverse solver and semantic lineages rather than one incumbent.
10. **Immutable Evidence Layer** — SHA-bound experiments, exact budgets, blind gates and independent runtimes.

- **Ω0 Scientific Lock** — mission, integrity rules, development/holdout boundary.
- **Ω1 Universal Substrate** — low-level deterministic typed IR, canonical serialization, executable semantics.

- **Ω6 Counterexample + Verifier Engine** — automated falsification and independent checking.
- **Ω7 Global Adapter Layer** — at least five heterogeneous ecosystems without solution-bearing adapters.
- **Ω8 Development Kill Campaign** — broad exposed-task attack; development may change Ω here.
- **Ω9 Final Freeze** — zero architecture revision budget after freeze.
- **Ω10 Global Blind Campaign** — large untouched multi-ecosystem denominator.
- **Ω11 Frontier + Reproduction Gate** — independent reproduction and external-frontier validation.

## Initial global success target

The final preregistration will refine exact numbers before holdout selection. The current design target is:

- at least 12 clean untouched discoveries;
- at least 8 causally attributable semantic-transfer wins;
- wins across at least 5 distinct ecosystems;
- causal credit for at least 4 independently induced semantic genes;
- decisive advantage over no-memory, equal-size random-memory, and static-search controls;
- zero post-freeze rescue;
- independent reproduction;
- at least 1–2 externally verifiable frontier advances.

These are development targets, not a retroactive gate and not a current result.
